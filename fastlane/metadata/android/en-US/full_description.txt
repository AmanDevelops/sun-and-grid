Sun & Grid is a local-first Android dashboard for a SolaX solar inverter with a Pocket Wi-Fi dongle. It shows live solar output, the panels-to-grid flow, today's and lifetime generation, and recent history.

The optional UPPCL section connects to a smart-meter account and shows month-to-date grid usage with a day-by-day calendar and a horizontally swipeable weekly chart. UPPCL credentials are stored on the device and sent only to uppcl.eeslsmartmeter.in.

Features:
- Today, Solar, Usage and Device tabs
- Live inverter readings over the local network
- A clear panels-to-grid flow card with 100% export
- Month-to-date grid usage and weekly chart
- Local reading history and optional 15-minute background sampling
- Light, dark and system themes
- Deterministic demo mode for trying every screen without hardware
- No ads, analytics, tracking or cloud account for solar data

The flow card is tailored to this grid-tied installation, where all generated solar is exported to the grid. Positive grid power is treated as export and can be swapped in settings for other firmware.

Sun & Grid is not affiliated with SolaX Power or UPPCL. Use it with hardware you own, keep the inverter dongle on your local network, and never expose it to the internet.
